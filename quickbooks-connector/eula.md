## 1. What this application is

`thornfield-reeves` is a private, internal software integration operated by Thornfield Industries. It connects a locally hosted [Model Context Protocol server](https://github.com/intuit/quickbooks-online-mcp-server) (published by Intuit) to a single QuickBooks Online company owned and administered by the same individual who operates the application.
