# Privacy Policy — Thornfield Google Workspace Assistant

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

## 1. Scope

This policy covers the Thornfield Google Workspace Assistant, a private integration operated by Thornfield Industries. Its sole user is its owner, who is also the data controller, acting on the owner's own Google accounts and household accounts that the owner administers.

## 2. What Google user data is accessed

With the owner's consent, given separately for each account, the application may access:

- **Gmail:** reading messages, applying and managing labels, and preparing drafts.
- **Calendar:** reading calendars, and creating events and invitations at the owner's instruction.
- **Contacts, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides:** read-only access.
- **Basic profile:** the account's email address and name, to identify which account is connected.

The exact permissions are shown on Google's consent screen when each account is connected.

## 3. How the data is used

Google user data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks the owner's AI assistants to perform: answering questions, organising mail, preparing drafts for the owner to review, and arranging calendar entries. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the owner's request.

Data is processed by AI models (Anthropic's Claude, and other AI tools the owner uses) acting on the owner's behalf, under the owner's own service agreements with those providers.

## 4. How the data is stored

OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's own hardware, with access restricted to the owner. Google user data is processed transiently, to complete the task in hand. It is not copied into a separate database by the application, except for notes the owner explicitly asks an assistant to keep in the owner's private records.

## 5. Sharing

No Google user data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. Nothing is transferred except as needed to perform the owner's request through the AI tools named in section 3.

## 6. Limited Use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Task data is not retained once the task is complete, apart from the owner-directed notes described in section 4, which the owner can delete at any time. Removing the application's access deletes its stored credentials for that account.

## 8. Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's [third-party connections page](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). That invalidates the application's tokens.

## 9. People mentioned in the data

People whose details appear in the owner's mail, calendars or documents may raise any privacy enquiry as an issue on [this repository](https://github.com/thornfield-industries/legal/issues).

## 10. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page, with the date above updated.
