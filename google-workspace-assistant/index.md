# Thornfield Google Workspace Assistant

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

The Thornfield Google Workspace Assistant is a **private integration** operated by Thornfield Industries for one person: its owner. It lets the owner's own AI assistants work with the owner's own Google accounts (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides) on the owner's behalf.

## Who uses it

Only the owner, using the owner's own Google accounts and the owner's household accounts that the owner administers. It is not offered to the public, is not available for sign-up, and is used by fewer than 100 users.

## Where it runs

On the owner's own hardware: a personal laptop and a home server. No hosted service sits between the owner's computers and Google.

## What it does

At the owner's instruction, the assistants can:

- read mail, calendars, contacts and documents, to answer the owner's questions;
- organise mail with labels;
- prepare email drafts for the owner to review and send;
- create calendar events and invitations the owner asks for.

The assistants do not send email on their own.
