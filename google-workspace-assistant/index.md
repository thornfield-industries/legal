# Thornfield Google Workspace Assistant

The Thornfield Google Workspace Assistant is a private integration operated by Thornfield Industries for internal use. It is not offered to the public.

It lets authorised users work with their own Google account data (mail, calendar, contacts and documents) through assistant tooling, at their own direction.
